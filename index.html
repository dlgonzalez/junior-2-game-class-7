Mission briefing

Someone is missing. Something went wrong.

Your teams are mountain rescue crews. A group of young hikers has a problem in the forest. To unlock the rescue route, teams must communicate, move, make choices, and use useful English.

🎯 Your missionFind clues, solve language challenges and rescue the hikers.
🗣️ English firstSay your answer aloud. Teammates can help, but the team must decide together.
🤝 Whole-class momentsSome challenges give points only when everyone participates.
Teacher: Put students in 3 teams. Choose a team captain for each round. Project the screen. No phones needed. For movement rounds, clear a small safe space at the front of the room.

How to play

Four missions. One rescue.

1. Take turnsTeams rotate. The active team answers first.
2. SpeakAnswers count when students say the English aloud.
3. HelpSome rounds let another team give a clue for bonus points.

Teacher controls the pace. You can accept natural A2 answers when the meaning is clear.

Mission 2 · Base Camp

Pack the rescue bag.

Team Camp's turn

A rescue bag is almost empty. The team has 30 seconds to make useful sentences. Pick a prompt, then send one student to the “bag” zone to act it out or point to an imaginary object.

🎒 BAG ZONE

something

🗣️ SAY IT

Make a sentence with the word.

Movement idea: students can mime the meaning, point to a classroom object, or act out a tiny scene. Keep the English short and natural.

Mission 3 · The storm

Everyone helps!

Whole class challenge

The storm has mixed up the rescue radio messages. The class must rebuild each message. First, everyone stands. Then the teacher points to students to form the sentence in the correct order.

Whole-class rule: if the sentence is correct, every team gets +5. If the class can also make a second true sentence with the same key word, every team gets +5 more.

Final Rescue · Mountain radio

Can you save the team?

The final radio message is yours. Teams have 45 seconds to create a mini rescue plan using at least three of today's useful words.

Situation: It is getting dark. Someone is hurt. The phone is not working. The rescue team is somewhere nearby. You need to tell them what to do.
